Emirhan Cam received his B.Sc. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Yıldız Technical University and is currently pursuing his M.Sc. degree in Electronics Engineering at Sabancı University. During his undergraduate studies, he gained experience in analog RF and millimeter-wave integrated circuit design through academic projects and research activities. His previous work includes the design of millimeter-wave LNAs and various analog integrated circuits using CMOS technologies. His current research interests focus on trusted millimeter-wave integrated circuits.
